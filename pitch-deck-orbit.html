How it works

One rule, running in the background.

Bank spend→ Plaid detects→ Orbit earmarks→ Threshold→ On-chain vault→ Yield

Before the threshold, the set-aside is just a number — the dollars stay in your bank. Plaid only watches; the money moves once, in a batch. Only the fiat→USDC hop (Stripe) is mocked for the hackathon — everything else is real.

Speaker · objection prep
"Why not move money per purchase?" — fees. Batching at a threshold means one conversion, not one per $5 coffee. "Is Plaid moving money?" — no, detection only; Stripe is the pipe, and it's the single mocked hop. Point at the 2% rate and "$50 → $1.00" on the screen.
