# Orbit — Website Demo Walkthrough

**Length:** ~3 min at a natural pace. **60–90s cut:** keep beats **0, 5, 9, 10** (hook, live demo, yield, on-chain proof).

Walks the site start to finish: landing page → into the app. Each beat has **[DO]** (what to show) and **[SAY]** (what to narrate).

Two things to check before you present:
- **Beat 10's numbers** (1,000,000 → 1,000,000.03) come from the demo vault. Make sure that's what's actually on screen when you open the explorer.
- If you're **not** using the demo account, swap beat 7's line for your real sign-in.

---

## 0 · Open — the hook (15s)
**[DO]** Land on the homepage `/`. Hero reads *"Money that grows itself."*
**[SAY]**
> "This is Orbit. The simplest way I can say it: it's a savings account that fills itself up and actually earns. You spend like normal, Orbit quietly sets a little aside, and then it grows that money with real yield, all without you lifting a finger. Think Acorns, but you own the money the whole time."

## 1 · Built on (10s)
**[DO]** Scroll to the *"Built on"* logo row.
**[SAY]**
> "It runs on Solana and plugs into the most trusted names in the space, so the money's moving on real, audited rails, not a black box."

## 2 · Your money at work (20s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"Your money at work" → "What your savings earn, invested on-chain."* Point at the per-year yield figure.
**[SAY]**
> "Here's the difference. A normal bank pays you almost nothing. Park $10,000 and you earn about $40 a year. Orbit puts that same money to work on-chain, so it earns real yield instead of sitting still and losing to inflation."

## 3 · Why Orbit (25s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"Why Orbit" → "What makes Orbit different."* Walk across the feature cards.
**[SAY]**
> "Three things make Orbit different. One, it's automatic: saving happens in the background, no willpower required. Two, it's yours: the money lives in a vault only you can touch, not ours. And three, you never have to understand crypto. No wallet to set up, no seed phrase, no jargon. It just works like a normal app."

## 4 · How your money stays safe (20s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"How your money stays safe."*
**[SAY]**
> "People's first question is always 'is my money safe?' Here's the honest answer. Your bank stays your bank until the moment you've actually saved enough to move. Nothing happens per-purchase; before that threshold it's just a number. And once it does move, it goes into a vault you own and can prove is yours, on-chain, anytime."

## 5 · See for yourself (25s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"See for yourself."* Trigger the interactive demo (simulated purchase → set-aside → deposit).
**[SAY]**
> "You don't have to take my word for it, you can watch the whole thing happen right here. I make a purchase. Orbit detects it and sets a slice aside. And once it crosses the threshold, it converts to digital dollars and lands in the on-chain vault. That's the entire engine, live."

## 6 · How much you save (15s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"How much you save" → "Put your spare change to work."* Nudge the savings chart.
**[SAY]**
> "And it adds up faster than people expect. A dollar a day of spare change, growing with yield, quietly becomes a real cushion, without you ever feeling it leave your account."

## 7 · Into the app (20s)
**[DO]** Click **Launch app**. On the auth screen, use the **demo login** (or sign in).
**[SAY]**
> "Let me take you inside. Signing in is one tap, email, Google, or Apple. No wallet, no setup. For the demo I'll jump straight in."

## 8 · The dashboard (30s)
**[DO]** Land on `/app`. Point to **Total saved**, **In vault**, **Set aside**.
**[SAY]**
> "This is your home base. Up top: everything you've saved, what's already invested in your vault, and what's currently set aside waiting to move. One glance tells you exactly where your money is."

**[DO]** Scroll to *"How much you set aside"* and *"Auto-invest at threshold."*
**[SAY]**
> "You're in full control of the dials. This is the slice Orbit takes from your spending, and this is the threshold, the point where it auto-invests. Set it once and forget it."

## 9 · Your vault & yield (25s)
**[DO]** Scroll to *"Your vault & yield"* — **Total value**, **Yield earned**, **Current yield** — then *"Where your USDC earns."*
**[SAY]**
> "Here's the part a bank can't show you. Your total value, the yield you've actually earned, and the rate it's earning right now, updating live. And it's completely transparent: you can see exactly where your dollars are working and the return they're getting."

## 10 · Prove it's real — the money shot (20s)
**[DO]** Click *"Open your vault to see it live on Solana."* Show the explorer: deposited **1,000,000.00** → withdrew **1,000,000.03**.
**[SAY]**
> "And this is the whole pitch in one screen. This isn't a mockup, it's a real vault on Solana. We deposited money, it earned, and we withdrew more than we put in. More out than in, a real transaction, public for anyone to verify. The yield is real and the money is provably yours."

## 11 · Activity + close (15s)
**[DO]** Click the **Activity** tab, scroll the feed, open one entry.
**[SAY]**
> "Every move is logged here, every set-aside, every deposit, and you can tap into any one for the full detail. That's Orbit: you spend like normal, it saves and invests for you, it pays real yield, and you own every dollar the entire time. Saving that finally runs itself."
